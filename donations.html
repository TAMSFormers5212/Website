Sponsor TAMSformers

TAMSformers is a high school FRC robotics team based in Denton, TX. We build competitive robots, mentor younger students, and inspire the next generation of engineers. Your sponsorship directly funds robot parts, competition entry fees, travel, and STEM outreach programs in our community.

We offer several sponsorship tiers to support our robotics team. Each tier comes with increasing visibility and engagement benefits. Choose the tier that fits your organization best.

Why Sponsor Us?

Sponsoring TAMSformers is an investment in the future of STEM education. Our team of dedicated high school students designs, builds, and programs a competitive robot each season while developing real-world engineering, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Your sponsorship helps us cover competition entry fees, robot parts and materials, travel expenses, and community outreach programs. In return, your organization gains visibility among students, families, educators, and the broader FRC robotics community.

We are proud to partner with organizations that share our passion for engineering, innovation, and empowering the next generation of problem-solvers.
