Description as published on tams.club:

The Repair Society will host an open house in the makerspace once a month. People bring in broken things and we help fix them on the spot, working through the repair with them instead of just taking the item and handing it back. Between events, people can request a repair anytime through an online form. The two committee heads run everything. This is based on the repair café model that a lot of schools and libraries already use, where about 62% of items people bring in end up getting fixed.
