REEFSCAPE, the 2025 game, asked robots to move Coral from the Coral Station
onto the branches of the Reef, clear Algae off it, and climb onto the Barge at
the end of the match.

Our robot collects Coral with a compliant-wheel intake and raises it on a
chain-driven elevator to the level being scored. The photos show it in the pit
and on the field at competition.
