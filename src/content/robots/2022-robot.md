Rapid React asked robots to pick Cargo up off the floor, feed it upward, and
score it into a raised Hub, then finish the match by hanging from a set of bars
suspended above the field.

Our entry took Cargo in over a low intake fitted with green compliant wheels
and carried it up an inclined conveyor to the scoring position, so the robot
could collect and shoot without having to line up precisely. A pair of hooks
mounted above the frame handled the endgame climb on the Hangar.
